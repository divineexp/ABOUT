Our Background

We started by extending a helping hand. Everything we've built since has been an extension of that.

Our journey began as a media company. During the pandemic, we stepped in to help nonprofits and others in need — connecting organizations with the people they served when that connection mattered most.

Service has always been our core intent. As creatives, design came naturally. One project became the next — websites, business cards, business launches — and our work expanded alongside our clients' needs.

Our education and extensive background in creativity, media, and understanding people form the foundation of everything we do today. We don't just design — we listen, we understand, and we build with intention.

Our Journey
Year 01
Where It Started

We began helping nonprofits with their media needs — fostering connections with their patrons and communities at a time when that work was needed most.

Year 02
Growing With Our Clients

Our clientele expanded and we added more services. Websites, branding, business launches — each one an act of service, each one sharpening our craft.

Year 03
Full Scale

We transitioned to full-time and turned DVX into a full-scale creative agency — formalizing our process, deepening our focus, and committing completely to the work.
